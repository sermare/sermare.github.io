Inverse folding models track structural compatibility in pMHC-TCR complexes
Sergio E. Mares, Brian Petersen, Kyle Barlow
Adimab, 2026
pdf / code
We sample peptides from four inverse folding models across 43 pMHC-TCR crystal structures and score them for predicted presentation and structural compatibility. Rather than recovering the native peptide or ranking binding strength, the models sample the sequence space compatible with the structural context — a probe of the tolerated neighborhood that drives cross-reactivity in TCR-based therapies.
Continued domain-specific pre-training of protein language models for pMHC-I binding prediction
Sergio E. Mares, Ariel Espinoza, Nilah M. Ioannidis
Machine Learning in Computational Biology (MLCB), 2025
proceedings / code
We test whether domain-specific continued pre-training of protein language models is beneficial for pMHC-I binding affinity prediction. Starting from ESM Cambrian (300M parameters), we perform masked-language modeling on HLA-associated peptides and fine-tune for quantitative IC50 binding affinity prediction.
