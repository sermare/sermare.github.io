Key Findings

Phase 1 (Negative): ESMFold TTT doesn't improve DockQ despite reducing perplexity. Folding head not trained on complexes.
Phase 2 (BREAKTHROUGH): lr=0.05 produces dramatic DockQ gains — 11/13 non-OOM pairs improved. Best: 1CGI 0.018→0.375 (ipTM=0.983). Consistent pattern: loss diverges past ~5, ipTM spikes to 0.2-0.98, DockQ follows 1-2 steps later. 1B6C peaked at step 30 (DockQ=0.244) then crashed — early stopping at peak ipTM may be key. 4 pairs OOM (>500 residues).
Hypothesis: TTT only helps when the downstream task head is competent.
