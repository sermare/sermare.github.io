Key Findings

Phase 1 -- Negative:
ESMFold TTT does not help. The folding head was never trained on complexes, so no amount of representation improvement can fix structural incompetence. Masked MSA loss decreases, but DockQ stays near zero.
Phase 2 -- Positive:
Evoformer TTT with lr=0.05 gives +924% mean DockQ improvement. 7/19 pairs cross the DockQ=0.23 "acceptable" threshold. The structure module, already trained on complexes, converts improved representations into better docking.
Insight:
Loss divergence at steps 30-40 triggers an ipTM spike and DockQ improvement. The model "breaks through" a local minimum. Early stopping at peak ipTM may further improve results.
